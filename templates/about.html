About Geoscience

Geoscience is the study of the Earth, including its composition, structure, physical properties, and processes. It encompasses various fields such as geology, geophysics, geochemistry, hydrology, and environmental science.

Geoscientists work to understand natural phenomena like earthquakes, volcanic eruptions, and climate change, while also exploring resources such as minerals, oil, and groundwater. By integrating observations, experiments, and modeling, geoscience helps society make informed decisions about sustainability and natural hazards.

Our platform, GeoGBT, aims to provide a comprehensive hub for geoscience knowledge, research, and interactive tools. Users can ask questions, access educational resources, and explore geoscience concepts through multimedia content.
